# Privacy Policy for ApiClient (iOS)

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2021  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

Abcoderz Software ("we", "our", or "us") built the **ApiClient** app for iOS as an ad-supported application with optional in-app upgrades. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information for anyone who uses ApiClient on iOS.

---

### 1. Local-First & Private by Design

* **All API Data Stays on Device** — Your requests, responses, headers, API keys, tokens, environment variables, dynamic variables, and session cookies are stored strictly on your local iPhone/iPad.
* **No Middleman Servers** — We operate no servers that intercept, collect, or store your API traffic. We have no technical ability to access or view your API calls.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for advertising and crash diagnostics:

* **Google AdMob** — Used to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers (IDFA) and diagnostics.  
  [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en)
* **Firebase Crashlytics** — Used to collect anonymized crash logs to fix bugs.  
  [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/)

---

### 3. Log Data

Whenever you use our Service, in case of an error or crash in the app, we collect diagnostic data (through third-party SDKs) called **Log Data**. This may include device name, iOS version, app version, time and date of the incident, and crash stack traces.

---

### 4. Cookies & Session Storage

ApiClient includes a built-in local Cookie Manager to assist developers in testing and inspecting API response cookies (`Set-Cookie`). All cookies captured during testing remain stored strictly inside the app's local sandbox on your device.

---

### 5. Security

We value your trust in using ApiClient. We protect your data using iOS security sandboxing. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we advise caution when connecting to untrusted networks.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
