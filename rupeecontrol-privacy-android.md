# Privacy Policy for Rupee Control (Android)

**Last updated:** August 2026

Abcoderz Software ("we", "our", or "us") built the **Rupee Control** app as a free application with an optional one-time Pro upgrade.

---

### 1. 100% On-Device Financial Data Storage

* **Local Storage Only** — All transactions, budgets, Khata records, and accounts are stored **strictly on your local device** using encrypted local storage.
* **No Server Uploads** — We do not transmit, sync, track, sell, or store your financial data on external servers.
* **Smart Notification Auto-Tracker** — Notification parsing happens locally on your device in real-time. No SMS or notification text is uploaded.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services

* **Google Play Billing** — For secure in-app purchases (Rupee Control Pro).
* **Google AdMob** — For banner and interstitial ads.
* **Firebase Crashlytics** — For anonymous crash diagnostics.

---

### 3. Data Deletion

* You can clear all data instantly in app settings or Android App Settings.
* Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data.
