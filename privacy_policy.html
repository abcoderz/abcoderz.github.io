Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 8 September 2026

FindEverything ("we", "our", "us") is a hyperlocal noticeboard that lets people post what they are looking for and lets others nearby respond. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It applies to the FindEverything Android application and this website.

FindEverything is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact the Grievance Officer below and we will remove it.

2. What Other Users Can See

Your mobile number is never shown to other users and is not part of your public profile. It is stored in a private record that only you and our moderation systems can read.

Other signed-in users can see: your name, your profile photo, your selected city, your posts and comments, and any business name, contact number or address you chose to add to your profile. People you message can see your messages.

3. Location

We request precise location only at the moment you create a post, and only to record where that post is, so that people searching nearby can find it. We do not track your location in the background and we do not build a location history. You may refuse the permission; posting still works.

4. Automated Content Moderation

To keep the service usable and lawful, post text and images, and comment text, are checked automatically before or shortly after publication. This check is performed by Google's Gemini API acting as our processor. Content that is flagged is withheld or removed and a record is retained as evidence.

An automated decision can be wrong. If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, contact the Grievance Officer and a person will review it.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

  • Other users, as described in section 2.
  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, push notifications, crash reporting, analytics, hosting) and the Gemini API (content moderation), acting as our processors.
  • Law enforcement or courts, where we are legally required to disclose, or where disclosure is necessary to protect someone's safety.

Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and may be processed outside India.

7. Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and equivalent laws elsewhere, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it — your name, photo, city and business details are editable in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and associated data. Settings → Delete Account removes it immediately, or you may request deletion at the address below.
  • Withdraw consent, including location permission, in your device settings. Notifications can be turned off in the app.
  • Complain to the Grievance Officer, and thereafter to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access is enforced by server-side security rules that restrict each record to the people entitled to it — in particular, your mobile number and notification token are readable only by you. App integrity is verified before a device may reach our data. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users and the Data Protection Board of a breach as required by law.
