# Privacy Policy for Rupee Control (iOS)

**Last updated:** September 2026

Abcoderz Software ("we", "our", or "us") built the **Rupee Control** app for iOS as an ad-supported application with an optional one-time Pro upgrade. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

---

### 1. Your Financial Data Stays 100% on Your iPhone

* **Local Storage Only** — Every transaction, category budget, debt & loan ledger (Khata) record, note, receipt photo, and cash counter total is stored **only on your iPhone**.
* **Encrypted at Rest by iOS** — The local database uses iOS Data Protection (`completeUntilFirstUserAuthentication`), so the system encrypts it on disk when the device is locked.
* **No Servers, No Accounts** — We operate no servers for this app. Your financial data is never transmitted, backed up to our servers, sold, or shared.
* **Biometric Lock** — You can require Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode to open the app.

---

### 2. Advertising

Rupee Control is supported by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**:
* **App Tracking Transparency** — iOS requests permission before tracking. Declining tracking does not affect app functionality.
* **EEA/UK Consent** — A Google user consent form is presented in regions requiring consent.
* **Removing Ads** — The one-time **Rupee Control Pro** purchase permanently removes all ads.
* Learn more: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [AdMob Policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

---

### 3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by **Apple** via StoreKit. We never see or store your payment details.

---

### 4. Diagnostics & Analytics

The iOS version of Rupee Control contains **no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs**.

---

### 5. Data Deletion

* You can delete individual entries or accounts anytime from within the app.
* **Deleting the app permanently deletes all stored data and receipt photos.**
