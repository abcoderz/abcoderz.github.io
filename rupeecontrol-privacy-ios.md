# Privacy Policy for Rupee Control (iOS)

**Last updated:** September 2, 2026

abcoderz ("we", "our", or "us") built the **Rupee Control** app for iOS as an
ad-supported application with an optional one-time Pro upgrade. This service is
provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and
disclosure of information for anyone who uses Rupee Control on iPhone.

> **Note on platforms.** This policy covers the **iOS** app. The Android version
> uses different services and is covered by its own policy.

---

### 1. Your financial data stays on your device

Rupee Control is built so that your financial records never reach us.

* **Local storage only.** Every transaction, income and expense entry, category
  budget, debt & loan ledger (Khata) record, note, receipt image and account is
  stored **only on your iPhone**, in the app's private storage.
* **Encrypted at rest by iOS.** The database is stored with iOS Data Protection
  enabled (`completeUntilFirstUserAuthentication`), so the operating system
  encrypts it on disk and it cannot be read while the device is locked and
  powered off.
* **No servers, no account, no sign-in.** We operate no server for this app.
  There is no account to create. Your financial data is never transmitted,
  synced, backed up to us, sold, or shared. We have no technical ability to see
  it.
* **Optional biometric lock.** You can require Face ID, Touch ID or your device
  passcode to open the app. Authentication is performed by iOS; the app never
  receives your biometric data.

---

### 2. Information you choose to enter

* **Khata / Ledger contacts.** You may optionally record a person's name and
  phone number against a debt or loan entry. This is stored on your device only
  and used solely to display the entry and, if you choose, to pre-fill a
  reminder.
* **WhatsApp reminders.** If you tap the WhatsApp reminder action, the app opens
  WhatsApp with a message already written for you. Nothing is sent unless **you**
  send it, and the message is handled entirely by WhatsApp under its own privacy
  policy.
* **Calendar reminders.** If you add a due-date reminder, the app requests
  write-only calendar access and creates a single event. It cannot read your
  calendar.
* **Receipt photos.** Photos you attach are stored in the app's private storage
  on your device and are never uploaded.

---

### 3. Adding expenses from bank messages

iOS does not permit any app to read your SMS messages or other apps'
notifications. Rupee Control therefore never reads them. Instead:

* **Share sheet.** You can share a bank SMS or UPI receipt into Rupee Control.
  The text is parsed **on your device** to suggest an amount and merchant. It is
  not uploaded anywhere.
* **Clipboard detection (optional, off by default).** If you turn this on, the
  app checks the clipboard for a bank message when you open an entry screen.
  This is entirely on-device and can be switched off at any time.

---

### 4. Advertising

Rupee Control is free and supported by advertising. Ads are provided by
**Google AdMob**, which may collect and use device identifiers and advertising
data to select and measure ads.

* **App Tracking Transparency.** On first use, iOS will ask whether you allow
  the app to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.
  **If you decline, ads simply become less relevant** — nothing else changes,
  and it never affects your financial data, which stays on your device either
  way.
* **Consent in the EEA and UK.** Where required, a Google-provided consent form
  is shown before any ad is requested, and your choices are respected.
* **Attribution.** The app includes Apple's SKAdNetwork identifiers, which let
  ad attribution happen without identifying you.
* **Removing ads.** The one-time **Rupee Control Pro** purchase removes all ads
  permanently and unlocks Excel and CSV export.

Google's practices are described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
[AdMob partner policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

---

### 5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by **Apple** through the App Store using
StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment details. Whether you own Pro is
determined by asking the App Store, and that answer is stored only on your
device.

---

### 6. Diagnostics

The iOS version of Rupee Control includes **no third-party analytics or crash
reporting SDK**. We do not use Firebase, Crashlytics, or any analytics service.

If you have enabled *Share iPhone Analytics* in **Settings → Privacy & Security
→ Analytics & Improvements**, Apple may share anonymised crash reports with us
through App Store Connect. That is controlled by you in iOS Settings and
contains no financial data.

---

### 7. Backups you create

The app can export an encrypted backup file of your data. You choose where it is
saved — for example iCloud Drive or Files. Once saved, that file is under your
control and governed by wherever you put it. We never receive it.

Treat a backup file with the same care as the data inside it: anyone who obtains
the file and has the app can restore it.

---

### 8. Children's privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

---

### 9. Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you remain in complete control:

* Delete individual entries, categories, accounts or profiles from within the app.
* **Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes all of its local
  data**, including receipt images.

Backup files you have exported are not affected by deleting the app; delete
those yourself from wherever you saved them.
